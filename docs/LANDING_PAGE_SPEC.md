# WISECASH LANDING PAGE — MASTER DESIGN & INTERACTION SPECIFICATION

## 1. DESIGN DIRECTION
WiseCash should feel like a premium, established business product—not a typical AI-generated SaaS website.
The design direction is:
- Premium, Editorial, Minimal, Modern, Trustworthy
- African / Tanzanian business focused
- iOS-inspired, Photography-led, Product-led, Simple, Confident
- Big Image → Few Words → Product → Whitespace → Big Image → Few Words → Product

### Avoid:
- Generic SaaS templates, "vibecoded" websites, AI startup clichés
- Emerald green, teal, cyan, purple/blue SaaS gradients, neon colors
- Excessive cards and floating gradient blobs
- Walls of text or fake statistics ("Trusted by 10,000+ businesses" unless verified)

---

## 2. BRAND COLOR SYSTEM
Primary colors:
- **Primary Ink**: `#1A1D29` (Main headings, nav text, primary buttons, dark overlays)
- **Brand Amber**: `#D99A4E` (Small accents, active states, selected highlights)
- **Dark Amber**: `#B87A32` (Hover states, stronger amber emphasis)
- **Soft Amber**: `#FEF7E6` (Subtle highlighted areas, badge backgrounds)
- **Page Background**: `#F7F7F5` (Primary marketing page background)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Floating nav, screenshot containers, pricing cards)
- **Secondary Text**: `#6B7280` (Supporting copy, descriptions, metadata)
- **Border**: `#E5E7EB` (Subtle dividers and containers)

Ratio: 70–80% neutral surfaces, 15–20% photography/product imagery, 5% brand accent.

---

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY
- **Display Font**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Hero heading, major section headings, large editorial statements)
- **Body / UI Font**: `Instrument Sans` (Navigation, body text, buttons, labels, pricing, FAQ, footer)

Hierarchy:
- Hero: 72–96px desktop, 48–60px tablet, 42–50px mobile
- Section Heading: 56–72px desktop, 42–52px tablet, 36–44px mobile
- Subheading: 22–28px
- Body: 16–18px (line height 1.5–1.7)
- Small labels: 11–13px uppercase, letter-spacing ~0.12em
- Buttons: 15–16px medium weight

---

## 4. NAVIGATION
- Desktop: Transparent over hero at top. On scroll: floating translucent surface (`rgba(255,255,255,0.85)` with backdrop-blur 20px, 1px solid border, 18px radius, height 64px).
- Logo: `WISECASH`
- Links: Features, How it works, Pricing, FAQ
- Right: Log in, Start free
- Language switcher: `EN | SW` (English default, Swahili ready)

---

## 5. BUTTON SYSTEM
- **Primary**: Background `#1A1D29`, Text `#FFFFFF`, Height 52–56px, Radius 14–16px, subtle 2–3px upward hover shift.
- **Secondary**: Background transparent, Border 1px solid `#1A1D29`, Text `#1A1D29`, Height 52–56px, Radius 14–16px.

---
