In Tanzania, pricing a retail management system like WiseCash (Smart Money) depends heavily on whether you are selling it as a bespoke custom software project (exclusive build for one client), a turnkey package (software + hardware + setup), or a recurring SaaS/subscription.

WiseCash is not just a simple calculator; it has bilingual Swahili/English support, PWA offline capability, inventory, HR/payroll, P&L analytics, barcode scanning, thermal receipt printing, and role-based access control (which solves the #1 pain point in Tanzanian retail: wizi wa wafanyakazi / employee shrinkage).

3 SaaS / Subscription Model (Best Long-Term Strategy)

If you retain the intellectual property and license WiseCash to multiple shopkeepers as a service:

  • One-time Setup & Onboarding Fee: TZS 150,000 – 300,000
    (Covers setting up their branch, uploading inventory catalog, configuring printers, and training cashiers).
  • Monthly Subscription: TZS 35,000 – 70,000 / month per branch (~$15 – $28 USD).
  • Annual Subscription (with 2 months free): TZS 350,000 – 650,000 / year.

4 Optional Add-ons & Recurring Services

  • Monthly Support & Cloud Backup Retainer: TZS 50,000 – 100,000 / month
  • Bulk SMS Integration (Beem / NextSMS): TZS 30 – 50 per SMS (for customer receipts and promotional alerts)
  • TRA VFD / EFD Fiscal Integration: If you integrate virtual fiscal device APIs (TRA E-Tax / VFD via approved vendors), you can easily charge an additional TZS 500,000 – 1,200,000 setup fee plus recurring compliance fees.

🏆 Key Value Points to Pitch to Tanzanian Clients

  1. Kiswahili Interface: Makes cashier onboarding instant with zero language barrier.
  2. Offline Mode (PWA): Works seamlessly during power cuts (Tanesco outages) and internet drops.
  3. M-Pesa / Tigo Pesa / Airtel Money Payment Categorization: Tracks mobile money transactions cleanly without balancing discrepancies at the end of the day.
  4. Audit Trail & Role Locks: Cashiers cannot delete sales or manipulate stock without manager/admin permissions.
